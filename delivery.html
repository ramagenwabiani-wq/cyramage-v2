Delivery Information

Everything you need to know about getting your CyRamage order, from dispatch to your door.

Delivery Options & Timeframes

Service Delivery Time Cost
Standard Delivery 3–5 working days £3.99 — free on orders over £50
Express Delivery 1–2 working days £5.99

Which Option Should I Choose?

Standard Delivery is our default and best all-round choice for most orders — fully tracked, and free once your basket is over £50.

Express Delivery is worth choosing when timing matters — a gift with a date attached, or simply when you'd rather not wait.

Order Processing

Orders are packed and dispatched within 1–2 business days of purchase. You'll receive a confirmation email with tracking as soon as your order ships.

Payment

All orders are processed securely through Stripe. We never see or store your card details directly.

Packaging

Every order is carefully wrapped to protect your items and arrive looking as beautiful as they feel — because unboxing should be part of the luxury.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a tracking link by email. If you can't locate it, contact orders@cyramage.com and we'll help you track it down.

International Delivery

We're working on expanding delivery outside the UK. For enquiries, contact orders@cyramage.com.

Returns & Exchanges

See our Returns page for details on returning or exchanging an order.

Questions About Your Order?

Reach our order support team at orders@cyramage.com.
